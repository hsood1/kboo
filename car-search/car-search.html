Leasing 101
Who Pays for What?
Leasing = paying to use something you don't own. Here's exactly how it works.
Your Responsibility
💸 You pay for this
💳
Monthly Payments
Every month, on time. Missing payments damages credit and can trigger repossession — same stakes as an apartment.
🛡️
Full Coverage Insurance (Required)
Leasing company mandates comprehensive + collision — minimum liability not allowed. Must be active before you drive off the lot.
🔧
Routine Maintenance
Oil changes, tire rotations, wiper blades — yours. Toyota covers the first 2 yrs / 25K miles free on new cars; Hyundai 3 yrs / 36K.
⛽
Gas or Charging
Always yours. Hybrids save ~$40–60/mo vs gas-only. Tesla EVs ~$20–30/mo with home/work charging.
🛣️
Mileage Overages — Watch This
Standard = 10,000–12,000 mi/yr. Every mile over = $0.15–0.25 at lease end. Estimate real commuting before signing.
🗑️
Disposition Fee ($350–400) at Return
Charged when you hand the car back. Avoidable if you lease or buy again from the same brand immediately.
Covered For You
✅ Manufacturer & dealer pays
🔩
Mechanical Breakdowns (Warranty)
Engine, transmission, hybrid battery failures = warranty-covered. Toyota: 3yr/36K basic + 10yr/150K hybrid. Hyundai: 5yr/60K + 10yr/100K powertrain. Honda: 3yr/36K basic + 5yr/60K powertrain.
🔁
Recall Repairs
Safety recalls are 100% free — bring it in, they fix it, no cost, no hassle.
📱
Software & Tech Glitches
Infotainment bugs, sensor malfunctions — all warranty-covered. Just schedule a service appointment.
🏠
No Depreciation Stress
At lease end, just return the keys. No selling it, no trade-in hassle, no being stuck with an aging car.
🚨
Roadside Assistance (Included)
Toyota, Hyundai, and Honda include manufacturer roadside — flat tires, lockouts, towing. Separate from your insurance.

Shopping Strategy
Tips for September
How to get the best deal when you're ready to shop in ~3 months.
01
Time it right — end of quarter is king
September 30 = end of Q3. Salespeople have monthly and quarterly quotas. The last few days of September dealers are more willing to deal than almost any other time of year. Go then.
02
Honda Poway — trusted dealer
Multiple positive firsthand experiences at Honda Poway. For the Accord Hybrid this is the recommended starting point — familiarity with a dealer removes a lot of stress from the process.
03
Go inland for Toyota / Hyundai
Toyota of El Cajon, Mossy Toyota (Kearny Mesa), and Temecula Toyota discount more aggressively than coastal stores. A 30-min drive can save $1,500–3,000 on the same car.
04
Verify cooled seats on the specific car
Always confirm ventilated seats are on the actual unit — not just the trim level generally. Check the window sticker and feel for perforation holes in the seat fabric before signing anything.
05
Get insurance quotes before signing
Run Geico, Mercury, and Progressive quotes before the dealer appointment. Insurance must be active on delivery day for leased/financed cars. Know the cost before committing.
06
RAV4 / Accord: Use Costco Auto or TrueCar
Toyota and Honda don't discount off MSRP easily, but Costco Auto Program and TrueCar pre-negotiate pricing at participating dealers. Often saves $500–1,500 with zero haggling.
07
Leasing: negotiate the cap cost, not the payment
Every dollar off the vehicle's capitalized cost lowers your monthly payment. Don't let dealers make the monthly number the only focus. Negotiate the car price first, then apply lease terms.
08
Think honestly about mileage
San Diego is a driving city. 20 mi each way × 250 work days = 10,000 miles just commuting. Consider upgrading to 12K mi/yr on any lease if that sounds tight.
09
Must test drive the Tesla first
The Model 3 has zero physical buttons — climate, mirrors, wipers all live in the touchscreen. Charging situation is solved (work + home), but only a test drive will tell if the interface works for you day to day.
10
Buy in CA — anywhere SoCal is fine
You must register in California. Temecula, El Cajon, Chula Vista, National City, Mission Valley, Kearny Mesa — all fair game. Buying in-state handles DMV registration automatically.
